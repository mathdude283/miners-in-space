# NASA material bundled in Moonbound

## artemis2_farside_view.jpg and orientale_crop.jpg

- What it is: NASA's annotated view of the Moon's far side that NASA published for the Artemis II lunar flyby. It labels Orientale Basin, Ohm, Jackson, Aitken, Apollo Basin, Aristarchus Plateau and three unnamed fresh craters. It is NOT a photograph taken by the player, and it is not a raw instrument image: it is an annotated graphic.
- Credit line shown on the source page: **NASA**.
- Source record: the image on NASA's blog post "Artemis II Flight Day 6: Lunar Flyby Updates" (6 April 2026, page updated 7 April 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/missions/2026/04/06/artemis-ii-flight-day-6-lunar-flyby-updates/
- Original file: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image.png (3240 x 3240 PNG, about 18 MB). No PIA or other image ID is given on the page.
- What we changed: resized to 1024 px (full view) and cropped and resized to 640 px (Orientale area) as JPEGs on a black background. No labels were removed or added.
- Terms: NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the US; credit NASA; no implied endorsement; NASA insignia not used. See https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ . No separate licence statement appears on the source page; the team should confirm before release.
- Used for: the "NASA reference" panel on the Orientale report, so the player's SIMULATED photo can be compared with a REAL NASA view.
